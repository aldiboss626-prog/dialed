# Dialed — Pitch Document

---

## One-Sentence Pitch

> Dialed is the personal relationship manager that turns your network into a pipeline — for anyone whose career runs on who they know.

---

## Core Thesis

> Opportunities don't come from job boards. They come from people. Dialed is built on that truth.

Most people treat relationships and opportunities as separate things. They maintain contacts in one place, track jobs in another, and hope the two somehow connect. They never do.

Dialed is built around a different belief — every real opportunity in your career is powered by a relationship behind it. The internship came from the alumni you stayed in touch with. The interview came from the recruiter you followed up with twice. The referral came from the manager you grabbed coffee with six months ago.

**The full loop: Relationships → Opportunities → Outcomes.**

---

## The Problem

Your network is your most valuable professional asset. You know that. Everyone knows that. But knowing it doesn't mean you're actually maintaining it.

You have contacts scattered across LinkedIn, your phone, your inbox — people you've met at events, worked with, been introduced to — and most of them are slowly going cold right now while you're busy with everything else. You think about them when you need something. And by then, it's too late to not look transactional.

The tools that exist don't solve this:
- **LinkedIn** shows you who you know — it doesn't tell you who's going cold
- **Spreadsheets** require you to remember to open them
- **HubSpot / Salesforce** are built for sales teams, not individuals
- **Job tracking apps** have no idea who helped you get there

Nothing is built to connect the people in your life to the opportunities they create.

---

## What Dialed Does

Every person in your network gets a cadence based on how important they are to you. When a relationship starts going cold, Dialed escalates. A nudge first. Then a harder push. Then urgent — because the longer you wait, the harder it gets.

And if someone reaches out and you haven't replied, Dialed catches it through your Gmail before the relationship takes the hit. A dropped response is how most opportunities die quietly. Dialed makes sure that doesn't happen.

On the other side, every opportunity you're tracking is connected to the person who made it possible. You're not just managing a job search. You're watching your relationships turn into real outcomes in real time.

---

## The Result

You stay proactive instead of reactive. Your network stays warm. Your pipeline stays full. And when an opportunity lands, you know exactly who to thank — because Dialed showed you who to stay close to all along.

---

## The Market

This isn't just for students. Anyone whose career runs on relationships has this problem:

- **Early career (22–30)** — building their network from scratch, no system, relationships going cold as they job hop
- **Sales & business development** — need to stay warm with prospects without a full CRM
- **Freelancers & consultants** — their whole business is referrals, which dry up when they go quiet
- **Executives & managers** — large networks, zero time, can't afford to let the wrong relationship go cold
- **Entrepreneurs** — every investor, partner, and customer relationship has real dollar value attached

The student angle is a strong entry point — students are forming habits and building networks from zero. But the product scales naturally upward as they grow in their careers. You could own a generation from college all the way through their professional life.

---

## The Gap

The tools that exist are either too heavy (Salesforce, HubSpot — built for teams) or too passive (LinkedIn — shows you who exists, doesn't tell you who to call). Dialed sits in the middle — personal, proactive, and it actually comes after you.

---

## Closing Line

> *Other apps help you find opportunities. Dialed builds the relationships that create them.*
